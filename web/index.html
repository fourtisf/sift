Good questions.

Does it buy random tokens?

No. A token has to pass every rule you set and score above your AI threshold. With the default rules, about 1 in 11 new tokens gets bought. With the Careful style, fewer than 1 in 100.

Where does it buy?

On Robinhood Chain, directly on the Pons bonding curve or on Uniswap after a token graduates. You pick which.

Market order or limit order?

Both. Buy right away with a slippage cap, or wait for a dip you choose. Limit orders that don't fill cancel themselves after 30 minutes.

When does it sell?

On your plan: sell half at your first target, trail the rest, stop out at your loss limit, and leave immediately if the deployer starts selling.

Can I lose money?

Yes. Memecoins are very risky. Filters cut out a lot of bad trades, not all of them. Only fund your agent with ETH you can afford to lose.
